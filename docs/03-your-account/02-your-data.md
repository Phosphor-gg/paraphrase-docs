# Your recordings and data

Why the original recording is kept, how to delete it, and what is never done with it.

## Why the original recording is kept

Editing never changes your original recording, and re-editing is free because that original is still there to cut a different way. It is kept for as long as the recording is in your account.

Delete a recording and its original goes with it. Delete your account from Settings and your recordings go with it.

## What is never done with it

Nothing you submit is used to train any model, ours or anybody else's, and it is not sold or licensed. Transcription and the cleanup both run on machines we operate.

The [privacy policy](/privacy) has the full detail.
