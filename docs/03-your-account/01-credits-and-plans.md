# Credits, plans and limits

What a credit buys, what each plan includes, and the limits on each.

## Credits

One credit transcribes one second of audio. There is one transcription model and no multiplier, so the cost of a recording depends only on its length.

Credits are spent once, when a recording is transcribed. Editing, cleaning up and exporting a recording you have already transcribed cost no credits.

Premium includes 120 hours of audio a month, which resets each billing period and does not roll over. Credit packs can be bought on either plan and do not expire while your account is open.

## Plan limits

| | Free | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI cleanups | 3 AI cleanups a day | 30 AI cleanups a day |
| Project length | projects of up to 5 minutes | projects of up to 4 hours |

A project's length is all of its recordings together. The daily cleanup count resets at midnight UTC.

When a free account reaches a limit, the message says which one and offers the upgrade. See [pricing](/pricing) for what Premium costs.
