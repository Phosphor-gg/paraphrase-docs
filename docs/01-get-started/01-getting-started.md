# Getting started

From a new account to your first exported edit.

## Your first edit

Sign in from the login page. A new account comes with fifteen minutes of credit, enough to transcribe and edit a short recording end to end, and no card is needed.

Everything you record lives in a project. Create one from the dashboard, give it a name, and open it.

Add a take to the project: record one in the browser, upload a file you already have, or paste a link to audio or video. The take is transcribed with a timestamp on every word.

Once the transcript is there, edit it. Press Clean up with AI to have the cleanup propose a cut, or click words to cut them yourself. When it reads right, export it.

## What an edit is

An edit is a choice of which of your words stay and in what order. The export cuts your original recording at those words and joins the pieces back up. Nothing is generated, so the result can only contain words you said.

## Getting help

Email [help@phosphor.gg](mailto:help@phosphor.gg), or ask in the [Paraphrase Discord](https://discord.com/invite/Ef36rWvCXm), whichever you prefer. If a take could not be processed, its project's editor says why, which tells you whether sending it again will help.
