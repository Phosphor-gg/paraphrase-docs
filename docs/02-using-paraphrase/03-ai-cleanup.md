# Clean up with AI

What the cleanup does, how to review it, and how many you can run.

## What it does

Clean up with AI reads the project's transcript the way an editor would and proposes the take you meant to record: filled pauses, false starts, hedges and the sentence you said twice taken out, and words moved where a sentence reads better.

It has no list of words to hunt for. Each word is judged by what it is doing in its sentence, and anything that carries meaning is kept.

## Reviewing what it did

The cleanup's cuts show in the transcript like your own, struck through. Put back any word it removed by clicking it.

It runs on machines we operate, and your transcript is not sent to a third-party model. It has been tested on English speech.

## How many you can run

The free plan includes 3 AI cleanups a day and Premium includes 30 AI cleanups a day. The count resets at midnight UTC. A cleanup that fails, or that the model could not answer, is not counted.
